## 🏆 Hackathon projects

### 🫁 Predict Asma — RESPIRA Hackathon (CIBERES) · October 2026
**A digital twin for severe asthma patients.**

- Estimates each patient's risk of asthma attacks and tells apart poor medication adherence from a treatment that simply isn't working.
- Modular Python pipeline over clinical data from 506 patients: data cleaning, a 30% blind test set, high-risk pattern detection and model training, run on the **MareNostrum 5** supercomputer.
- Web app for doctors and nurses to simulate interventions (quitting smoking, losing weight) and see their expected effect.
- Key finding: about 80% of patients who had attacks were taking their medication properly, pointing to a lack of drug efficacy.

`Python` `Machine Learning` `HPC / MareNostrum 5`
🔗 [Devpost](https://devpost.com/software/predict-asma)

### 🌴 JUNGLIX — bitsxlaMarató 2025 · December 2025
**A cooperative web game and chat for kids aged 12–16 who can't communicate directly, for example for health reasons.**

- Players move around a map completing mostly cooperative missions: answering questions, collecting items and helping secondary characters.
- A moderator (a responsible adult at the center) creates rooms and can give hints when things get hard.
- Node.js backend with MongoDB to manage rooms and users.

`JavaScript` `Node.js` `MongoDB` `HTML` `CSS`
🔗 [Devpost](https://devpost.com/software/junglix) · [Code](https://github.com/macelbarrio/HACKATON-JUNGLIX)
